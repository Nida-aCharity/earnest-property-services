About Earnest Property Services

Professional people behind better property decisions.

We are a professional property and real estate consultancy providing valuation, surveying, management, development and property research services across Uganda.

Who We Are

A trusted professional partner for property.

Earnest Property Services is a professional property and real estate consultancy serving individuals, businesses, investors, financial institutions and other organisations across Uganda.

Our work is focused on providing reliable professional information, technical property services and practical advice that helps clients make informed decisions about land and property.

From valuation and land surveying to property management, project management, development advisory and property research, we support clients across different stages of the property lifecycle.

Professional information. Practical advice. We combine technical knowledge, local market understanding and field experience to provide dependable property services.
